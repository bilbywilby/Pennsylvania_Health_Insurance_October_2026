## Legend & Status Codes

## Analytical Summary & Structural Observations

1. **Risk-Adjustment Pain & Above-Request Approvals:** 
   * All three carriers approved above request (HPP, KHPE, QCC) are southeastern Independence/Jefferson-adjacent entities where federal risk-adjustment data confirmed absorption of high-cost, chronically ill members. Regulators treated higher premiums as actuarially necessary to prevent insolvency.
2. **Rural Structural Failure vs. Urban Density ($RA_2$ vs. $RA_8$):**
   * **Rating Area 2 (North Central - Elk, Cameron, Potter):** Dominated almost exclusively by Geisinger and Highmark variants (Archetype C), leaving consumers vulnerable to the "Rural Cliff" and limited arbitrage alternatives.
   * **Rating Area 8 (Philadelphia Metro):** Features the highest carrier density in the Commonwealth (up to 14 active options), though middle-income households faced severe subsidy-cliff shocks.
3. **Enrollment Attrition & Adverse Selection Loop:**
   * Cumulative mid-year effectuated enrollment dropped to $443,024$, driven by over $160,000$ total cancellations following the expiration of Enhanced Premium Tax Credits (EPTCs) and a $102\%$ average net price shock. Healthy exits create a sicker residual pool, reinforcing upward rate pressures.
